### W1: Might have overlooked other features that explain linear separability.

**Response**: To ensure a balanced and well-established set of representation quality metrics, we follow the categorization proposed by Skean et al. (2025). To address your suggestion, we additionally include the **curvature** measure (Hosseini et al., 2023), which analyzes differences in hidden-state trajectories between human- and machine-generated text. We find that **human-written text exhibits higher curvature** than machine-generated text in the middle layers. We have included this metric and its analysis in the representation quality analysis in **Section 3.2**.

---

### W2: The study did not delve deeply into how the detection performance changes with the parameter scale of the LLM or the specific architectural design choices.

**Response:** In **Appendix D**, we already provide an extensive ablation study covering both (a) different language model families (Llama vs. Qwen) and (b) four model sizes up to 8B.

**Revision:** To address your suggestion, we have extended the ablation with two additional model sizes, **Qwen-0.6B** and **Qwen-32B**. As expected, we observe that **larger models consistently improve performance**, whereas the **smaller model reduces performance** compared to the 8B baseline. The table below summarizes the results:

| Model | FP | PE | SUM | TST |
|-------|---:|---:|----:|----:|
| Qwen-32B | +0.020 | +0.029 | +0.013 | +0.048 |
| Qwen-0.6B | -0.012 | -0.036 | -0.005 | -0.022 |

While our ablations already examine different model families and scales, we agree that evaluating **different architectural choices** is an interesting direction for future work and have added this to the **Conclusion**.

### W3: Benchmark comparison between it and specialized multi-classification or regression detection models.

**Revision:** We have added an extensive benchmark comparison between **EditLens**, a fine-tuned BERT model with a three-way classification head, and our two probing methods in **Appendix C.5**. Specifically, we train each model on the training set, tune the two decision thresholds on the validation set, and evaluate the resulting ternary classifier on the test set.

We report the results on the **EditLens** benchmark below. We find that **both probes achieve the highest overall performance** on the three-way AI-edit detection task while also maintaining **strong performance across all three classes**.

### W4: This study focuses solely on the most basic logistic regression linear detector.

**Response:** The overarching aim of our study is to demonstrate that **linear probing** provides strong OOD transfer and sample efficiency. Importantly, we show that **simple linear probes are sufficient** to outperform more expressive models, such as an MLP with non-linear transformations (Figure 2). While we adopt the standard linear probing setup proposed by Alain and Bengio (2016) and Nanda et al. (2023), we acknowledged this limitation in the **Limitations** section and added to the **Conclusion** that exploring alternative probing architectures is a **promising direction for future research**.
